Key Findings

Structure sharpens between epochs 100 and 160

The predictor's strong-edge AUROC rises from 0.66 (epoch 110) to 0.87 (epoch 150) and 0.90 (epoch 160). Before epoch 100 it sits near chance (0.45–0.62) and keeps 13–15 edges with 6–7 false positives. This is read by eye; the per-edge emergence epoch from the pre-set rule (first checkpoint kept at every later one) has not been computed yet.

F1 stabilizes late (epochs 150–200)

F1 is noisy in early training (0.45–0.61) because the model keeps many edges. After epoch 150, it stabilizes around 0.67–0.75. The untrained (epoch 0) predictor keeps 0 edges—it scores randomly.

False positives fall to zero

False positives decline from 7 (epoch 10) to 3 (epoch 140), 1 (epoch 150) and 0 from epoch 160 onward, while the number of kept edges falls from 14 to 5. The graph gets smaller and cleaner, not larger.

Only part of the strong graph is recovered

From epoch 160 on, the model keeps 5–7 edges, all true, out of 10 strong edges (F1 0.67–0.75). At 50 splits the missed edges were the pole-angle ones (θ→Δv̄, θ→Δθ, θ→Δω̄, ω̄→Δθ), which the method cannot separate because θ and ω̄ are highly collinear. Which edges are missed at each epoch of this 20-split curve is in each epoch's edges.md.

AUROC plateaus at about 0.92–0.94

AUROC peaks at 0.936 (epoch 180) and stays at 0.92–0.93 through epoch 200. F1 is flat at 0.67–0.75 over the same range, so F1 differences between late epochs (for example 0.75 at epoch 180 versus 0.67 at epoch 200) are one or two edges and should not be over-read. These are single-run results with one training seed.
